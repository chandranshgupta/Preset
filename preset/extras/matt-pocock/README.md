# extras/matt-pocock: unofficial reference material

These files come from a community write-up, **not from Matt Pocock and not from his repository**
(`mattpocock/skills`). They are examples that are **not installed** by `install.sh`: CI workflow
sketches, a personal-wiki template, a starter repo layout and a sources map.

Treat every claim in `INTEGRATION_GUIDE.md` and `REPOSITORIES_AND_SOURCES_MAP.md` about his repositories,
numbers of skills or his workflow as unverified. The authoritative source is
<https://github.com/mattpocock/skills> and the pinned copy described in `../../UPSTREAM.md`.
